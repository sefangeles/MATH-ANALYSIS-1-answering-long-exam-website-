Before you submit

  • Check that you differentiated the inside function when using the chain rule.
  • For implicit differentiation, remember that differentiating a term involving y introduces dy/dx.
  • For a normal line, its slope is the negative reciprocal of the tangent slope, when defined.
  • For logarithmic differentiation, differentiate ln(y) and then solve for y′.
